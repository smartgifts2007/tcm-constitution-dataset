# Global Online Distribution of the Nine TCM Body Constitutions (n = 21,242)

Open **CC BY 4.0** aggregate CSV dataset and methodology report from **21,242 completed 29-question assessments** (14,104 unique respondents across 164 countries and regions, `2026-03-17` to `2026-11-18`), scored under the official **Wang Qi / CACM nine-constitution framework** (`ZYYXH/T157-2009`).

- **Live Daily Source of Record:** https://www.identifytcm.com/constitution-statistics
- **Interactive 9-Constitution Reference:** https://www.identifytcm.com/constitution
- **Published Research Page (GitHub Pages):** https://smartgifts2007.github.io/tcm-constitution-dataset/

## Key Summary Findings

1. **Qi Stagnation (`qiyu`, 氣鬱質) ranks #1 overall (26.4%)** and leads every adult age band from `18–29` (`34.7%`) and `30–39` (`36.7%` peak) through `60+` (`23.0%`).
2. **Balanced (`pinghe`, 平和質) accounts for 6.2% overall** (~1 in 16 assessments, and `2.4%–3.1%` under age 40) in this self-selected online health-seeking cohort.
3. **Mixed Constitutions (43.2%):** In `9,171` of `21,234` scored assessments, the top two biased patterns sit within `≤ 5` points of each other, led by `Qi Deficiency + Qi Stagnation` (`13.2%` of near-ties).
